# Duke Macros

A macro tracker for Duke Dining. Menus and nutrition come from Duke's NetNutrition and are refreshed daily; items you can order ahead are tagged from the Duke Dine-Out (Mobile Order) app.

Your food log, saved meals and limits are stored only on your device. Use **Limits → Backup & transfer** to copy them.

Live app: https://nebil-s.github.io/duke-macros/
